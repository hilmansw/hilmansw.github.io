Honored to speak at a webinar titled “Learning Data Science in the Age of AI: What Skills Will the Industry Still Be Looking for in 2026?”, attended by more than 
70 participants. The session explored the evolving skills required to remain competitive in the Data Science field amid the rapid advancement 
of Artificial Intelligence. The discussion highlighted the importance of combining strong technical capabilities with adaptability, problem-solving,
and practical experience to meet industry demands. Participants were also encouraged to identify ways to differentiate themselves from other candidates 
by building relevant skills, developing meaningful portfolios, and demonstrating their ability to solve real-world problems.
