Honored to speak at a webinar titled “Artificial Intelligence in Research: The Use of Tools and Its Implication in Health,” 
attended by dozens of participants from the Ruang Research community. The session explored the growing role of Artificial Intelligence 
in supporting research activities, particularly through the use of AI-powered tools to enhance productivity, analysis, and knowledge 
discovery. The discussion also highlighted the implications of AI in the health sector, including its potential to support research, improve 
decision-making, and contribute to the development of innovative solutions. Participants were encouraged to understand both the opportunities 
and responsibilities associated with AI adoption in research and health. The session concluded by emphasizing that the use of AI should be guided 
by critical thinking, ethical considerations, and responsible practices to ensure that technological advancement creates meaningful benefits 
for research and society.
