Honored to speak at a webinar titled "Industrial Transformation Through Artificial Intelligence: Data Science as the Foundation 
of AI for the Industry of the Future", organized by RISTIE HMTE Universitas Brawijaya and attended by more than 40 students from 
several universities. The session explored the growing role of Artificial Intelligence in driving industrial transformation and 
highlighted Data Science as a fundamental foundation for developing effective AI solutions. The discussion emphasized the importance 
of understanding data, analytical thinking, and AI technologies to address real-world challenges and meet the evolving needs of future 
industries. Participants were encouraged to strengthen their knowledge and practical skills in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence 
to prepare themselves for the opportunities and challenges of an increasingly technology-driven industrial landscape. The session concluded 
by emphasizing that the future of industry will be shaped not only by advanced AI technologies, but also by the ability of future talents 
to transform data into meaningful insights and impactful solutions.
