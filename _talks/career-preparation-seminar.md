Honored to speak at the Career Preparation Seminar organized by the Student Association of Electrical Engineering, Telkom University Purwokerto, 
my alma mater during my undergraduate studies. The session, titled “The Rise of the AI Era: Intelligent Technology Transformation as an Opportunity 
for Career Development in the Digital Age,” explored the rapid advancement of Artificial Intelligence and emerging technologies. The discussion 
highlighted the importance of building strong foundations in Data Science, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI to remain relevant 
in an increasingly technology-driven workforce. Participants were encouraged to continuously develop their knowledge and skills, embrace technological 
adaptability, and explore how AI can create meaningful opportunities across various industries. The session concluded by emphasizing that AI should 
not be viewed as a replacement for human capabilities, but as a technology that can empower individuals to solve problems and create a meaningful impact.
