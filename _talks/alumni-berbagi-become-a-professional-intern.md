Honored to share my experiences and insights with the students of SMK Telkom Purwokerto as one of its alumni. The session explored my journey from studying at SMK Telkom Purwokerto to pursuing higher education, including the academic and non-academic experiences that shaped my personal and professional development. The discussion also highlighted my internship journey and experiences working as a teacher at SMK Telkom Purwokerto. Participants were encouraged to make the most of their learning opportunities, continuously develop their skills, and build experiences that can support their future academic and career journeys.
